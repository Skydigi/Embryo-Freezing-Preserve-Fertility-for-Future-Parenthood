## 1. What Is Embryo Freezing?

- Eggs are fertilised through IVF, and the resulting embryos are frozen for later use.
- Suited to people who want to delay pregnancy, or who face medical treatment that may affect fertility.
- Embryos can be stored for years and thawed for transfer when the patient is ready.

## 2. The Embryo Freezing Process

| Step | Stage | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ovarian stimulation | Fertility medicines help the ovaries produce several eggs in one cycle |
| 2 | Egg retrieval | Minimally invasive, ultrasound-guided outpatient procedure under sedation |
| 3 | Fertilisation | Conventional IVF or ICSI, using partner or donor sperm |
| 4 | Embryo culture | Embryos grow in the lab for ~3–5 days; the healthiest are selected |
| 5 | Cryopreservation | Vitrification (rapid freezing) to avoid ice-crystal damage |
| 6 | Storage | Kept in cryogenic tanks at very low temperatures until transfer |

---

## 3. Benefits

- **Higher success rates:** often better implantation than egg freezing alone
- **Flexibility:** long-term storage lets you plan a family on your own timeline
- **Fertility preservation:** useful before cancer treatment or other medical procedures
- **Genetic testing:** allows preimplantation genetic testing (PGT)
- **Cost-effectiveness:** often cheaper than repeating fresh IVF cycles

---

## 4. Embryo Freezing vs. Egg Freezing

| | Embryo freezing | Egg freezing |
|---|---|---|
| What's frozen | Fertilised embryos | Unfertilised eggs |
| Sperm needed now? | Yes (partner or donor) | No; fertilisation can be delayed |
| Success rate | Higher, since embryos are already developing | Slightly lower |

## 7. FAQs

**What is the best age to freeze embryos?**
Usually before 35, when egg and embryo quality tends to be higher. Freezing after 35 can still help, but success rates may be lower.

**Is embryo freezing painful?**
The freezing itself isn't. Hormone injections may cause mild discomfort or bloating. Egg retrieval is done under sedation, and any soreness or cramping afterwards is usually mild and brief.

**How long does the process take?**
About 2–3 weeks, including roughly 10–14 days of injections, then monitoring, retrieval and vitrification. (The page answers this for egg freezing.)
